Consequences & Health

Health Problems

The silent impact of bad habits on the human body

Maintaining a poor diet for a long time can cause serious health problems. The lack of vital nutrients and the excess of fats and sugars facilitate the emergence of several serious diseases that affect quality of life and longevity.

Major Diseases Related to Poor Nutrition

Understand what happens when the body does not receive the right fuel

01

Type 2 Diabetes

Occurs when blood sugar levels become too high. It is very common among those who consume excessive amounts of sweets and refined carbohydrates.

02

Anemia

Happens when the body does not receive the required amount of iron and vital vitamins, leaving the person weak, tired, and lacking energy for daily life.

03

High Blood Pressure (Hypertension)

Is directly linked to the excessive consumption of salt and sodium, found mainly in ready-made seasonings, ultra-processed foods, and industrial products.

04

Heart Problems

Occur due to the accumulation of fat in blood vessels from poor nutrition, which can clog arteries and drastically increase the risk of heart attacks.

05

Gastritis

Arises from eating at irregular times, skipping meals, or consuming too much fat and irritating condiments, directly affecting the stomach lining.

06

Obesity

Develops through the uncontrolled consumption of empty calories, sugars, and bad fats, leading to disproportionate weight gain and general body inflammation.

The Good News: Prevention is the Way

Small daily changes prevent major future complications

Most of these conditions can be avoided or controlled simply by adjusting what goes on your plate and adopting a more active routine. Prioritizing natural foods is the cheapest and most efficient investment in your longevity.
